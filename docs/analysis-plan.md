# Analysis Plan: E-commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 1. Business Objectives

Analyze historical e-commerce transactions to understand business performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

## 2. Sales Performance Analysis

Questions:
- What is the total revenue?
- How does revenue change monthly?
- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- How does order volume change over time?

KPIs:
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly Revenue Growth

## 3. Product Performance Analysis

Questions:
- Which products sell the most units?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products have low sales performance?

KPIs:
- Units Sold
- Product Revenue
- Number of Orders per Product

## 4. Customer Analysis

Questions:
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- How many customers make repeat purchases?
- What is the average revenue per customer?

KPIs:
- Total Customers
- Revenue per Customer
- Repeat Purchase Rate

## 5. Transaction Analysis

Questions:
- How many transactions are cancelled?
- What percentage of transactions contain negative quantities?
- Are there unusual transaction values?

KPIs:
- Total Transactions
- Cancellation Count
- Cancellation Rate

## 6. Analysis Tools

- Python: Data Cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis
- SQL: Business Queries and Aggregations
- Excel: Reporting and PivotTables
- Power BI: Interactive Dashboard

## 7. Expected Outcomes

- Identify major sales trends.
- Discover top-performing products.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify cancellation patterns.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.
